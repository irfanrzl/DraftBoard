How it works

From structure to interface

Draftboard reads a diagram and generates a real draft from it directly. Here's the whole path.

1

You give it a diagram

Three ways in: paste DBML or Mermaid text, upload a screenshot of an ERD or wireframe, or just describe what you want in plain English. Any combination works.

2

Gemini reads and classifies it

One call to Gemini 3.5 Flash reads your text and/or image directly — no separate parsing step. It decides what you're building: an ERD becomes a dashboard, a page layout becomes a website — and applies smart defaults as it goes: a status field becomes a colored badge, a created_at a sortable date, a foreign key a linked relation.

3

You get a working draft

A single, self-contained HTML page — list views with search and sort, detail views, and working create/edit/delete against realistic placeholder data. Preview it live, then download the file.

Two engines, one product

Dashboard engine

Turns a database schema (DBML, Mermaid, or a screenshot) into an interactive admin dashboard with full CRUD.

Website engine

Turns a page layout or a description into a multi-page website, and can theme it from a mockup image's colors and style.
